About Me

A little about who I am

I am a student who enjoys practical assignments, clear steps, and seeing how a digital tool can solve an everyday problem.

My Story

I am studying at Georgia State University and working on becoming more confident with technology. I did not begin this course knowing every program or shortcut. I have learned by following directions, trying different approaches, and paying attention to feedback.

The part I enjoy most is turning a rough idea into something organized. That could be a clean Word document, a spreadsheet that makes sense, a presentation that is easy to follow, or a website with working navigation.

What Matters to Me

I value work that is accurate, useful, and easy for another person to understand. I also believe that asking questions and revising weak areas are part of learning, not signs of failure.

Strengths I Am Building

  • Staying organized when a project has several parts
  • Following directions closely and checking details
  • Explaining ideas in simple, direct language
  • Learning new software through practice
  • Improving work after feedback

My Direction

What I want to do next

My immediate goal is to keep strengthening the computer skills that college and modern workplaces expect. I want to become faster with common programs while still producing careful work.

As I gain experience, I hope to use those skills in a role where organization, communication, and technology come together.
