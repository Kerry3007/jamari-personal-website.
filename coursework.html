Coursework

Work that helped me build stronger habits

These examples show more than a final file. They represent planning, practice, revision, and learning how to present information clearly.

Relevant Course

LT 2010: Computer Skills for the Information Age

The course focuses on practical computer use for school, work, and everyday problem solving.

WEB REVIEW

Website Evaluation

I reviewed examples of effective and ineffective websites. I paid attention to navigation, layout, clarity, graphics, consistency, and whether the pages worked as expected.

DOCUMENTS

Word Processing and Formatting

I practiced organizing written work with headings, spacing, page numbers, tables, and a consistent style. The main lesson was that formatting should help the reader, not distract from the message.

SPREADSHEETS

Working With Data

I used spreadsheets to arrange information, apply basic formulas, and present results in a readable form. Careful labels and checking the numbers were just as important as the formula itself.

SLIDES

Presentation Design

I learned to reduce crowded text, choose useful visuals, and keep a presentation consistent from one slide to the next. A strong presentation should support the speaker instead of repeating every sentence.

WEB PROJECT

Personal Website

I moved from a written outline to storyboards, a site map, page content, navigation, and testing. This project brought several course skills together in one final product.

Reflection

What changed in my work

Earlier in the course, I sometimes focused on finishing quickly. I now spend more time checking the purpose of the assignment, naming files clearly, and reviewing how another person will use what I created.

That small change has made my work easier to follow and easier to correct.
