My Approach

I learn best by doing the work, checking it, and improving it.

Technology makes more sense to me when I can use it for a real task. I try a tool, review the result, and fix what does not work. That process has helped me become more patient and organized.

My goal is not only to finish an assignment. I want to understand the steps well enough to use them again.
